machine. The rules of the three timers are in `bench/README.md` ("The
matmul timers"). The tables use the `cold` rows only. All times are the
median op time in µs.

- "Candle per row": `[M, 1, K]`, one matrix-vector launch for each row.
  "Candle mm": `[M, K]`, the matmul kernel. At M = 1 they are the same.
- "#3898": `[M, 1, K]` with the PR, one launch for up to 8 rows.
- The gap list uses the layout that Fornace uses: per row up to B = 4
  (`q8`) or B = 6 (`q4`), then mm. The reference is MLX for `bf16` and
  ggml for Q8_0 and Q4_0.
- Check of the ggml timer (`-test-backend-ops.txt`, `hot` mode, 0.5B):
  gate_up agrees within 10 %. For `lm_head`, the ggml timer is 13 to
  32 % slower than `test-backend-ops perf`. So the ggml reference here
  is, if anything, too slow.
